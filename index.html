About

Practical software, built with care.

Hamidullah is a full stack developer based in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, working primarily with Laravel and PHP. He focuses on real business applications — the kind that clinics, salons, restaurants, and shops depend on every day — rather than one-off demos.

His approach starts with the workflow: how appointments get booked, how orders move through a kitchen, how a store tracks stock. The code follows from there — clean, structured, and built to be maintained, not just shipped.

AI is part of the daily workflow, not an afterthought. Hamidullah uses AI tools — including the Claude, OpenAI, and Gemini APIs, plus AI coding assistants — to scaffold features, debug faster, and generate documentation, so more time goes into the parts of a project that actually need a human eye: structure, UX, and business logic.
